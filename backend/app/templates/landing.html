Round 1 opening positions

Answers first. Nothing else is in the room yet.

Geminiseat 2 · reads the question + Claude

Gets Claude's full answer as its own input, so it can pick the argument up instead of starting over.

ChatGPTseat 3 · reads everything above

Sees both turns and can side with one, or take apart both.

Round 2 cross-examination
Claudeseat 1 again

Now holds every turn from round 1. It is told to name the weakest claim in the room, challenge it, and say what it changed in its own position.

Verdict written by the chair
Chairreads the whole record

One model you nominate reads the entire transcript and writes the answer: the recommendation, the reasoning that survived, where the panel split, and what would change its mind.

Bring your own keys

Paste an Anthropic, OpenAI or Google key and it is encrypted before it reaches the database. Delete it any time. Calls are billed by the vendor directly to you.

Running something self-hosted? Point a custom agent at any OpenAI-compatible URL — Ollama, vLLM, OpenRouter, your own gateway.

What it costs you

A three-agent, two-round debate is seven model calls: six turns plus the verdict. Long transcripts mean long prompts, so keep groups small and rounds few unless the problem earns more.
