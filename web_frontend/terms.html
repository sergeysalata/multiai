What the service does

multiai.online lets you assemble a group of AI models from different vendors and hold one conversation with all of them. Each model reads what the others have said before replying. You can attach files, which are passed to the models in the room.

API keys and cost

The models are not ours and the calls are billed to you. You add your own Anthropic, OpenAI, Google or self-hosted API keys, and every turn any agent takes spends your credit with that vendor directly. This service adds no charge and takes no cut — but it also cannot refund what a conversation costs.

Two things run up a bill faster than people expect. Every agent is sent the whole conversation each time it speaks, so cost grows as a chat gets longer. And in flow mode the agents keep talking to each other without waiting for you. There are limits in place — a cap on turns, and stopping when the room has nothing left to add — but you are responsible for what you start. Watch the first few runs before leaving one going.

What the models say is not advice

Language models produce confident text that is sometimes wrong. Putting several of them in a room helps — disagreement surfaces errors that a single model would state flatly — but agreement between three models is not proof of anything. They can all be wrong in the same direction, and often are, because they were trained on overlapping data.

Nothing produced here is professional advice: not medical, legal, financial, or engineering advice. Check anything that matters before acting on it, especially anything involving safety, money or the law.
